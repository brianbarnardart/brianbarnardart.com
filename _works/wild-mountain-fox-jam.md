An original acrylic work blending the natural world with myth and imagination.  The wide open sky is mirrored in the feet of the fox, giving him an irresistable cosmic quality.  Trees are recognizable despite being little more than neon blurs lit by starlight.  It's a psychedelic scene which encodes the signature Brian Barnard style.

The Fox Jam is his best known work and calling card...if for no other reason than it became his *literal* calling card!  (If you've visited Brian's table during a live painting session, you've likely been offered a free postcard of the piece as a promotional item.)

> "When I was seeing how popular [Wild Mountain Fox Jam] turned out to be, it became clear that **people really like animals** in the paintings.  And then among animals, foxes have a fairly universal visual appeal.  So it was double-popular."
>
> "Postcards were a bit more expensive than business cards, but if you buy them by the thousands it's not bad.  I  figured people could actually use them, and it was good advertising.  So to make a long story short: I don't think I'm ever going to have a business card!"
>
> — Brian Barnard
