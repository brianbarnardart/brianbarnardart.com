An original acrylic work blending the natural world with myth and imagination.  The wide open sky is mirrored in the feet of the fox, giving him an irresistable cosmic quality.  Trees are recognizable despite being little more than neon blurs lit by starlight.

This psychedelic scene encodes the signature Brian Barnard style.  It's his best known work and calling card...if for no other reason than it became his *literal* calling card!  Visitors to Brian's table during a live painting session can pick up a free postcard of the piece as a promotional item.

---

> "Wild Mountain Fox Jam was created in 2019 and displayed at Rain Dogs in a group Art Show that year.  Rain Dogs was one of the fixtures of Five Points in the Riverside area of Jacksonville."
>
> "This piece represents nature, music and magic: three topics that are common in my work.  The Nymph brings music into the world with her spirit guide the Fox, while watching over the magic of the natural world."
>
> "Being from Florida, mountainous environments are extra magical to me!"
