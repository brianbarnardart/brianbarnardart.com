This 2020 painting is an essential capture of Brian's "Flower Nymph" archetype.  The bright colors of the ornamental headdress compellingly suggest flowers, dessert, hair, and dripping paint... all at once.  With a contented expression, she takes it all in.

The characteristic slanted mouth and closed eyes appear in many later works, as another touchstone of recognizing when a piece is "a Brian Barnard".
