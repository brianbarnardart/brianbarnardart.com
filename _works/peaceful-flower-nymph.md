This 2020 painting is an essential capture of Brian's "Flower Nymph" archetype, whose characteristic slanted mouth and closed eyes became iconic in later works.  The bright colors of the ornamental headdress suggests flowers, dessert, hair, and dripping paint at once.  With a contented expression, she takes it all in!
