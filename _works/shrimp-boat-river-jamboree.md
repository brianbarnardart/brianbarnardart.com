**[This is a "diptych"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diptych)**: two large panels that can be positioned however you like *(on the same wall, on opposing walls, on a corner...)*  The suggestion is to mount them with a bit of a gap--to make a kind of implicit frame between them.  But they're shown here directly adjacent, so you can see the how the blue and brown tones coordinate.

The work was started in 2025 with the intention to be displayed at a business.  But that business's ownership changed, which left the unfinished pieces in limbo.  They were picked back up and reimagined to be completed in time for the 2026 Fernandina Shrimp Festival.
