**The Big Lebowski** [(wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Lebowski) occupies its own strange corner of cinema.  It shouldn't hold together as well as it does, but is built on a deadpan absurdism that rewards repeated viewing. The Coen Brothers' meditation on bowling, rugs, and the philosophy of doing nothing particularly well has accumulated a genuine cult following and a rich visual identity.

The film's color palette, its mix of grandiosity and squalor, and the way it finds meaning in the meandering all resonate with Brian's sensibility.  It's one of his favorite movies, and he's done several paintings of characters and scenes.
